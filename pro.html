Why Upgrade to FrankPass Pro?

The free website is a great standalone generator. The Pro Extension is built to eliminate daily friction, protect you against phishing, and supercharge your workflow.

Zero Typing Friction

1-Click Toolbar Auto-Fill

Save 15+ minutes every day. The extension detects the active tab and fills your mathematically generated password in 0.1 seconds. No more manually switching tabs or copy-pasting.

Cryptographic Domain Isolation

Mathematical Anti-Phishing Defense

Because FrankPass generates passwords strictly using the active website domain as cryptographic input, a fake clone site (such as g00gle.com or flipkart-deals.in) automatically receives a completely different, unrelated password. Your real password for Google or Flipkart is never generated or leaked to the attacker.

Keep your work and personal life neatly segregated. Switch between Personal, Office, Freelance, and Client vaults with a single click.

Hardware Security

Mathematical Immunity

Zero risk of central server hacks. FrankPass Pro computes passwords locally on your CPU using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA512 with 1,000,000 iterations. There is no server database to breach, and your secrets never leave your device.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers about how FrankPass Pro works.

Q: When will the Pro extension be released and how much will it cost?

Pricing will be decided later and officially announced on our X account (@frankpasshq) and WhatsApp Channel. Community members will receive honest pricing without recurring traps.

Q: Does FrankPass Pro store my passwords or master secrets on a server?

Never. FrankPass Pro is 100% deterministic and stateless. Every password is mathematically computed on the fly on your local device CPU using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA512. We have zero user accounts and zero password databases.

Q: Can I use FrankPass Pro completely offline without internet?

Yes. Once the extension is installed in your browser, it runs 100% offline forever. You can disconnect your Wi-Fi or turn on Airplane Mode, and it will still generate and auto-fill your passwords seamlessly.

Q: Can I use my license on multiple browsers and computers?

Yes. Your license allows you to activate FrankPass Pro on all your personal computers across Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Brave browsers.
