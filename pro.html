Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers about how FrankPass Pro works.

Q: Does FrankPass Pro store my passwords or master secrets on a server?

Never. FrankPass Pro is 100% deterministic and stateless. Every password is mathematically computed on the fly on your local device's CPU using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA512. We have zero user accounts and zero password databases.

Q: How do I receive and activate my Pro extension license?

Immediately upon checkout on FrankBase Store (store.frankbase.com), your instant license key is displayed on screen and sent to your email. Simply paste the key into the extension toolbar popup to unlock all Pro features.

Q: Can I use FrankPass Pro completely offline without internet?

Yes! Once the extension is installed in your browser, it runs 100% offline forever. You can disconnect your Wi-Fi or turn on Airplane Mode, and it will still generate and auto-fill your passwords seamlessly.

Q: Can I use my license on multiple browsers and computers?

Yes. Your license allows you to activate FrankPass Pro on all your personal computers across Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Brave browsers.
