Q: If I change my PC or my motherboard burns, will I lose my passwords or profiles?

Never! Your Master Secret Passphrase mathematically derives the exact identical password on any computer or browser in the world on frankpass.com or any extension, as long as your Master Passphrase, Service Name, and 4-Digit PIN match. Additionally, our 1-Click Encrypted JSON Backup Export/Import allows you to transfer 5 or 50 custom profiles to any new PC in 1 second without retyping them manually. The license key is bound to the old physical motherboard, but your passwords and JSON profiles remain 100% under your sovereign control.

Q: What does 'Physical PC Motherboard Lifetime' mean?

All FrankPass Pro plans are valid for as long as that physical computer's motherboard remains operational (whether it runs for 10, 20, or 50+ years). Reinstalling Windows/macOS, formatting your SSD, or updating Chrome does not alter your Device ID. If a motherboard burns out, loyal buyers receive our locked rate on Sovereign Store for exactly 1 replacement PC.

Q: Can I test FrankPass Pro before purchasing?

Yes, 100% Free! You can generate a 7-Day Diamond Pro Offline Trial Key instantly at store.frankbase.com/frankpasspro-extension-trial without providing an email, phone number, or payment card.

Q: Does FrankPass Pro store my passwords or master secrets on a server?

Never. FrankPass Pro is 100% deterministic and stateless. Every password is mathematically computed on the fly on your local device CPU using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA512. We have zero user accounts and zero password databases.

Q: Can I use FrankPass Pro completely offline without internet?

Yes. Once the extension is installed in your browser, it runs 100% offline without remote network dependencies. You can disconnect your Wi-Fi or turn on Airplane Mode, and it will still generate and auto-fill your passwords seamlessly.
