Questions & Objections Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about mathematical self-custody vs traditional password managers.

How is FrankPass fundamentally different from traditional cloud-based password managers?
Traditional managers act as cloud storage lockers: they save your passwords into an encrypted database file on their remote servers. If their servers are breached, your vault is exposed. FrankPass is not a vault; it is a deterministic mathematical engine. It calculates passwords on-demand using 1,000,000 rounds of PBKDF2-SHA512. When you close your browser, credentials vanish from RAM. We cannot be breached because we hold 0 bytes of your data.
If FrankPass does not store my passwords anywhere, how does it know my password when I return?
Through pure deterministic mathematics. Just as 2 + 2 always equals 4 anywhere in the universe, combining your Master Secret Key with the website domain (e.g. google.com) will always produce the exact same 16 to 32-character password. You do not need a centralized database when you have math.
What happens if I lose my internet connection?
FrankPass operates with 100% functionality with zero internet. Because it runs locally inside your browser or desktop using the WebCrypto API, it never sends a single network packet. You can generate, verify, and autofill credentials in an airplane, in a basement, or during a global cloud outage.
Why is a one-time purchase safer than a monthly subscription?
Subscription managers must maintain multi-region cloud servers, sync databases, and telemetry clusters, forcing them to charge monthly fees and constantly creating cyberattack surfaces. FrankPass has zero server overhead for credential handling. We charge an honest one-time lifetime license, aligning our incentives directly with your privacy, not recurring billing.
Can background malware or clipboard sniffers steal my password?
With legacy managers, yes: most tools copy plaintext passwords to the operating system clipboard buffer where background malware easily captures them. FrankPass Pro features Zero-Clipboard Autofill, injecting credentials directly into the webpage DOM node without ever touching the OS clipboard buffer.
Can someone brute-force my Master Secret Key if they know my generated password?
No. FrankPass utilizes PBKDF2 with 1,000,000 iterations and SHA-512 cryptographic hashing. Reversing a 1-million-round one-way cryptographic derivation would require centuries of supercomputing power, making brute-force attacks mathematically infeasible.
What if a website forces me to change my password periodically?
Simply update the rotation tag. You can increment the number from 1 to 2, or use natural months and years like Jan2026. Your master secret key remains completely unchanged, while the generator calculates an entirely fresh, mathematically unique high-entropy password for that specific site.
Will typos in my platform name or email result in a different password?
No. FrankPass features a built-in Smart Input Normalization Engine. For emails, it automatically strips accidental whitespace and converts all characters to lowercase. For websites, our extensive platform dataset automatically resolves domains and URLs (e.g. sbi.co.in or https://retail.onlinesbi.sbi/) into a single canonical seed shown transparently below the input field, guaranteeing consistent password generation across all your devices.

💡 Choose an Unpredictable Sentence:
Invent a private 5 to 8 word phrase (e.g. the blue giraffe ordered hot espresso at dawn). Avoid obvious names or dates.
✨ Case & Space Normalization:
MY PHRASE 2026 = my phrase 2026 = myphrase2026. Spaces and capital letters are automatically normalized across devices.
