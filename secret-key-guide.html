💡 What is a Master Secret Key in Plain Words?

Your Master Secret Key is like a private mathematical signature that exists only in your memory. It is never stored on any server, never saved in a database, and never sent across the internet.

Instead of saving 100 different passwords in an online cloud vault (which can get hacked), you remember just one memorable secret phrase. FrankPass uses pure client-side mathematics (1,000,000 PBKDF2 rounds) to instantly calculate a completely different, unhackable password for every website you visit.

The Golden Formula:

[Website Name] + [Your Secret Key] = Unique Unbreakable Password (Always 100% same on all your devices, 100% offline).

Good vs. Bad Secret Keys: The FrankPass Reality

Important: Your Secret Key is not your final password-FrankPass converts whatever you type into an unbreakable 80-bit military-grade password. So what truly matters is easy personal memory!

❌ Hard-to-Remember & Scrambled Keys (Avoid)

In FrankPass, a "Bad Secret Key" is simply anything you are likely to forget or make typing mistakes on:

  • abcxyzcxbc (Random scrambled letters you will forget)
  • qzwxplkjhf987 (Gibberish with no meaning to your brain)
  • P@$$w0rd!#%9 (Trying to type complex passwords into the key box)
  • 198765432100 (Long random numbers with zero natural hook)
🛡️ Accidental Special Characters?
Don't worry if you accidentally add symbols (like ! or @). FrankPass's Smart Normalization Engine automatically cleans symbols and spaces in RAM, so you always derive the exact same password without typos.
✅ Natural, Memorable & Unbreakable Keys (Recommended)

Any natural sentence, family bond phrase, or simple 1–2 words you know by heart:

  • i am king of my family (Confident sentence)
  • rohit priya aarav 2022 (Name + Spouse + Child/Year)
  • mastermanikant is an honest person (Truth statement)
  • morning walk with hot coffee (Daily habit)
  • two hot samosas with green chutney (Food memory)
  • blue lotus or peaceful (Simple 1–2 words)
✨ The 2-Way Customization Rule:
Even if you choose a 1-word Secret Key, you can make your password 100% unique by doing any one of these:
  1. Change the Password Number (Rotation Counter) (e.g. from 1 to 2).
  2. Enter your Email or Username in the generator.
Either method guarantees a 100% unique, uncrackable password that never collides with anyone else!

🔢 Password Number (1, 2, 3...) Made Effortless

No confusing technical words. Here are the 3 simple rules for the Password Number field:

Rule 1: Always Keep It 1

For 99.9% of your daily logins, keep the number set to 1. You will never have to remember any numbers.

Rule 2: Make It 2, 3 on Request

If a website forces you to change your password (or there was a news of a breach), change the number from 1 to 2. You get a brand-new password without touching your Secret Key!

Rule 3: Month/Year for Office

Only use the Month/Year mode (e.g. 10/2026) if your company/corporate IT enforces a strict monthly password expiry policy.

Platform Blueprint: Email vs. Username vs. Blank

"Should I enter my email or username?" Here is the definitive guide for every major platform so you never get locked out.

If you have a single account (which is best for the vast majority of users who have only one account on a platform-like a single Facebook account, single Gmail, or single Instagram), keep the Username field BLANK. If you have more than one account on the same platform (e.g. personal vs. business), enter your username or email-just remember whether you filled this field or left it blank!

Core Security Principles & Golden Rules

Essential knowledge for protecting your accounts and mastering stateless cryptography.

✨ The 1–2 Word Secret Key Rule

If you choose a short 1-word or 2-word Secret Key (e.g. peaceful or blue lotus), always enter your Email or Username in the generator's Username field. Because your email address is globally unique to you, FrankPass blends your unique email with the 1-word key across 1,000,000 PBKDF2 rounds, producing a 100% unique password that will never collide with or match anyone else's password.

🛡️ Data Breach Irreversibility

If a third-party website is breached and their database leaks your generated password, attackers can never reverse or extract your Master Secret Key. PBKDF2 with HMAC-SHA512 is cryptographically one-way. A breach on one platform reveals zero information about your passwords on any other platform.

💳 Universal Key for Both Passwords & ATM/UPI PINs

You can use the exact same Master Secret Key on the FrankPass PIN Generator. The underlying mathematical salt and context vector are completely isolated, ensuring that your 4-digit ATM PIN has zero cryptographic overlap with your 16-character web passwords.

📓 The Zero-Knowledge Physical Backup

Because FrankPass stores zero data on any server, there is no "Forgot Password" backdoor. When first establishing your new Secret Key, write it down in a physical diary kept locked safely at home until you have it 100% memorized.
