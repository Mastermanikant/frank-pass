💡 What is a Master Secret Key & How to Create the Best One?

Your Master Secret Key is not your final password - it is a private sentence created by you that generates all of your passwords. You keep it for as long as you use FrankPass and never need to change it, so take a moment to pick a strong, comfortable one from the start.

Its strength comes from being impossible for anyone else to guess while staying effortless for you to remember forever. Aim for at least 8 words and include a number (for extra unpredictability, you can even place the same number at both the beginning and the end).

To make typing effortless, FrankPass ignores spaces between words and capital vs. small letters. Only letters (A-Z, a-z) and numbers (0-9) are kept - everything else is removed automatically.

⚠️ Important Safety Rule: Never copy any example from this guide word for word. Public examples are known to everyone - always invent your own private sentence.

How Passwords Are Derived & Why Backup Matters

[Platform Domain] + [Your Master Secret Key] + [Counter] = Unique Strong Password (100% reproducible offline on any device).

If you are certain you will never forget your sentence, you do not have to write it down. Just keep in mind that if you ever forget your Master Secret Key, you will not be able to regenerate those passwords and will have to reset them one by one on each website. When using the FrankPass App or Extension, you rarely type it daily - so picking an unforgettable surreal sentence or keeping a quiet offline note in a diary is always a smart safeguard.

🧪 Spacing & Capitalization Rule (Normalization Example)

✅ Effortless Recall

Normalization ensures a long passphrase is easy to remember and never produces a different password due to accidental spaces or capital letters - you get the exact same password for each platform every single time.

Example (For Understanding Only): Suppose you choose the sentence: "Frankpass creator Master Manikant Yadav is my friend"
1. With Capital Letters & Spaces:
Frankpass creator Master Manikant Yadav is my friend
2. All Lowercase & Spaces:
frankpass creator master manikant yadav is my friend
3. All Lowercase Without Spaces:
frankpasscreatormastermanikantyadavismyfriend
✅ Result: Regardless of which variation you type, FrankPass derives the exact same Master Secret Key and generates the 100% identical password every time - just keep the spelling and word order identical. Changing word order (for example, is my friend manikant) creates a different Master Secret Key and a different password.

Good vs. Bad Secret Keys: The FrankPass Reality

Your Secret Key is not your final password. FrankPass converts your input into a high-entropy password using 1M PBKDF2 rounds. What matters is easy personal memory + unguessability.

❌ Avoid: High Risk of Forgetting or Dictionary Attacks

Avoid anything easily guessed via OSINT or prone to typing errors:

  • abcxyzcxbc (Random scrambled letters you will forget)
  • qzwxplkjhf987 (Gibberish with no meaning to your brain)
  • P@$$0rd!#% (Avoid special characters: symbols are stripped in RAM leaving only p0rd, easily cracked by dictionary attacks)
  • 198765432100 (Long random numbers with zero natural hook)
  • hello / india / manikant (Common single words in dictionary lists)
🛡️ Typing Protection:
If you accidentally add punctuation symbols, FrankPass Normalization strips them in RAM, preventing broken passwords.

✨ Password Rotation:
No need to use numbers sequentially (1 to 2). You can change the Password Number (1 to 999999) right from the start to any number (e.g. 100, 742) or choose a month/year variant (e.g. May 2046) for double-layer security without changing your Master Key!
