💡 What is a Master Secret Key in Plain Words?

Your Master Secret Key is a private mathematical signature that exists only in your memory. It is never stored on any server, never saved in a database, and never sent across the internet.

Instead of saving 100 different passwords in an online cloud vault (which can get breached), you remember just one memorable secret phrase. FrankPass uses pure client-side mathematics (1,000,000 PBKDF2 rounds) to instantly calculate a distinct, high-entropy password for every website you visit.

🔬 The Truth: Output Search Space vs. Master Secret Entropy

✅ The generated output possesses an 80-bit maximum combinatorial search space (Base-32, 16 characters):

7m3kq8p2v9w4nx6j

Fundamental Cryptographic Law: 1,000,000 rounds of PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA512 make offline cracking computationally exhausting for attackers. However, a KDF cannot synthesize entropy from nothing. Your effective security is strictly bounded by:
Effective Security = min( Hsecret, Hsearch )
If you pick a weak 1-word secret like apple, an attacker targeting dictionary lists can still guess it. Your real-world security is bounded by the linguistic entropy of your Master Secret Key.

🤔 Why Should Your Secret Key Be Long & Surreal?

The risk is not what your output password looks like, but whether an attacker can guess your Master Secret Key and regenerate your credentials on their own device.

❌ Short / Common Secret Key

An attacker who knows you use FrankPass could test common dictionary words on their own hardware. Common single words have low entropy and can be systematically checked.

✅ Surreal Multi-Word Secret Key
40 penon wala hathi aasman me udta hai 100

A private, non-dictionary multi-word phrase significantly reduces dictionary vulnerability. Offline guessing becomes computationally costly under 1,000,000 PBKDF2 rounds.

  • Extension PIN Synergy: You only memorize this story once. FrankPass Extension encrypts the key locally on your device behind a 4-digit PIN (default), 6-digit PIN, or password for 1-click daily convenience.

⚙️ Smart Normalization: The Engineering Trade-Off

1. Why FrankPass Normalizes Inputs: FrankPass automatically converts inputs to lowercase and strips non-alphanumeric characters and spaces via rawVal.toLowerCase().replace(/[^a-z0-9]/g, ''). This eliminates mobile keyboard auto-capitalization errors, smart punctuation discrepancies, and keyboard layout bugs.

2. The Character Pool Trade-Off: Normalization reduces the per-character alphabet size from ~94 (all printable ASCII) to 36 (a-z, 0-9). To maintain top-tier cryptographic strength, users should compensate for the reduced alphabet by choosing longer, multi-word passphrases (6 to 9 words).

3. Think Native, Type Roman: Because non-ASCII symbols are stripped, type your native language sentences using standard Roman script (Hinglish): e.g., hawa me tairti hui chaay ki pyali 500. This ensures 100% deterministic output on any machine worldwide.

🛡️ FrankPass Threat Boundary: What It Stops vs. What It Cannot

Scientific cybersecurity demands transparent boundary definition. Stateless cryptography protects sovereign credential generation, but cannot defy endpoint malware physics:

✅ Threats FrankPass Is Designed to Prevent or Materially Reduce
  • Centralized Cloud Vault Breaches: No master passwords or encrypted vault files exist on any server to be compromised.
  • Cross-Site Credential Stuffing: A data breach on one website never exposes or affects passwords on other platforms.
  • OS Clipboard Interception: The FrankPass Extension injects credentials directly into DOM input nodes without touching the operating system clipboard buffer (reducing exposure to standard clipboard sniffers).
  • Subscription Hostage: Never get locked out of your passwords due to billing disputes, server downtime, or service shutdown.
⚠️ Threats Beyond Mathematical Boundary (User Responsibility)
  • Compromised Host Device: Active kernel keyloggers or malware recording keystrokes while you type your secret key on an infected machine.
  • Deceptive Phishing Domains: If tricked into typing credentials on a phishing page, FrankPass derives a domain-specific password, but the target password may still be exposed if manually typed.
  • Forgotten Secret Key: Forgetting your master passphrase means permanent loss of regenerated credentials.
  • Physical Shoulder Surfing: Observers watching your keyboard while entering master passphrases in public without privacy screens.

🔢 Password Number (1 to 999999) Made Effortless

No complex technical jargon. Here are the 3 simple rules for the Password Number field:

Rule 1: Always Keep It 1

For standard daily logins, keep the number set to 1. You will never have to track varying numbers.

Rule 2: Increment on Rotation (2, 3...)

If a website forces a password change (or after a known website security incident), increment the number from 1 to 2 (up to 999999) to derive a brand-new password without altering your Secret Key.

Rule 3: Month/Year for Corporate Expiry

Use the Month/Year mode (e.g. 10/2026) only if your enterprise IT enforces strict 90-day expiry schedules.

Core Security Principles & Technical Truths

Essential knowledge for protecting your accounts and understanding deterministic cryptography.

✨ The Role of Email / Username in Derivation

Entering an Email or Username binds the derivation specifically to your account entity, preventing password collisions with other users on the same website. Note: Because email addresses are public identifiers, adding an email does not substitute for a high-entropy master passphrase against targeted adversaries.

🛡️ Data Breach Irreversibility

If a third-party website is breached and their database leaks your derived password, attackers cannot reverse or extract your Master Secret Key. PBKDF2 with HMAC-SHA512 is cryptographically one-way. A breach on one platform yields zero cryptographic information about your passwords on any other platform.

💳 Universal Key for Both Passwords & ATM/UPI PINs

You can use the same Master Secret Key on the FrankPass PIN Generator. The underlying mathematical salt and context vector are strictly isolated, ensuring that your 4-digit ATM PIN has zero cryptographic overlap with your 16-character web passwords.

📓 The Zero-Knowledge Physical Backup

Because FrankPass stores zero data on any server, there is no "Forgot Password" backdoor. When first establishing your new Secret Key, write it down in a physical diary kept locked safely at home until you have it 100% memorized.
