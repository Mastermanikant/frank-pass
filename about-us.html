Platform Mission & Principles

Privacy is a Human Right.

We built FrankPass on an absolute conviction: individuals must be able to secure their entire digital presence without trusting any cloud corporation with their cryptographic secrets.

Our 3 Sovereign Promises

Uncompromising architectural guarantees that govern the FrankPass platform.

🔒

1. Zero Data Storage & Zero Backdoors

Your passwords and secrets are never stored on any server, database, or disk file. Everything is calculated transiently in RAM via native WebCrypto API. We have no "Forgot Password" backdoor because mathematical truth needs no administrative master key.

🎁

2. 100% Lifetime Free Core & Free Extension

Security must never be locked behind a paywall. The web generator, offline mobile PWA, banking PIN calculator, and Browser Extension Guest Mode autofill are 100% free with zero mandatory fees for every human on Earth.

⚖️

3. Absolute Transparency & Zero Tracking

Zero third-party trackers, zero Google Analytics, zero advertising networks, and zero fake marketing counters. Every mathematical equation is open, auditable, and documented in RFC specifications.
