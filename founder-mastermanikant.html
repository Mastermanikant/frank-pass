25 December Genesis: The Origin of FrankPass

On the night of 25th December 2025, while managing 20+ YouTube and Gmail accounts, I was locked out of a vital account after forgetting its complex password. Frustrated by endless recovery loops, the core realization hit me: humans cannot remember 50 random passwords, and storing them in cloud databases only creates targets for hackers.

That night, I asked myself a simple question: "Why store passwords anywhere when pure mathematics can generate them on-demand from a single secret phrase?" That insight gave birth to FrankPass (originally codenamed IRY PASS) — a pure, stateless cryptographic utility with zero servers and zero database breach risks.
