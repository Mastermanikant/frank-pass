How to Use FrankPass

Just 4 simple steps to generate your stateless passwords.

Step 1

Open frankpass.com

Navigate to frankpass.com on any browser - Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or any modern mobile browser. No app store required.

Step 2

Enter Platform & Secret Key

Type the site name (e.g. "instagram" or "gmail.com") and choose a memorable secret key - a phrase, lyric, or pattern only you know. This never leaves your device.

Step 3

Click Generate

Hit the Generate button. Your password appears instantly - computed entirely in your browser using PBKDF2 + HMAC-SHA512. No server call is ever made.

Step 4

Copy & Use Your Password

Click Copy and paste it into your login form. For maximum security on shared devices, remember to clear your clipboard after logging in. Never paste your password on untrusted sites.

Masterclass & Best Practices

How to Master Stateless Security

Three essential habits that guarantee your digital identity remains 100% unbreakable forever.

💡

1. Choose a Natural Sentence

Never use a short word or birthday. Instead, pick a natural memorable sentence with 4+ words that only you know:

✓ Good: i love drinking chai at 7am
✗ Bad: password123 or manikant@1995
🗂️

2. Multi-Tier Secret Keys

Compartmentalize your risk by using 2-3 different memorable sentences for different life domains:

  • Tier 1 (Everyday Social): For Reddit, forums, casual media.
  • Tier 2 (Work & SaaS): For GitHub, Slack, work emails.
  • Tier 3 (Banking & Primary): For net banking and recovery Gmail.
🔄

3. Password Rotation via Counter

When a website forces you to change your password every 90 days, you don't need to change your master secret key:

Simply increment the Variant Counter from 1 to 2 (or 3). The math automatically derives a completely new high-entropy string!
