01 USERNAME / EMAIL LOGIC

When To Fill & When To Leave Blank

Stateless mathematical modifier. Know the exact derivation effect:

💡

Leave Blank for 90% of Platforms

For single-account services (Netflix, Amazon, LinkedIn), leave blank. It eliminates the hassle of remembering email IDs during password recalculation.

👥

Fill Only for Multi-Account Isolation

If you hold 2+ accounts on one site (Instagram Personal vs Creator, or Gmail Work vs Personal), enter username to generate distinct passwords.

🔒

Zero Server Storage Guarantee

FrankPass never stores your username on any server or database. It is processed in local RAM as an ephemeral PBKDF2 hash input.
