The Mathematics of the 32-Character Invariant

Why did we choose exactly 32 characters for the default clean mode? In computer science and information theory, $32 = 2^5$. When generating random indices from binary entropy pools, a 32-character alphabet eliminates modulo bias completely. Each character represents exactly 5 bits of uniform entropy.
